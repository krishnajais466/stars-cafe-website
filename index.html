About Star's Cafe

Star's Cafe has been serving Nalasopara East since 2022 — a veg cafe where friends and families come for pizza, coffee, freakshakes and birthdays. Dine in, take away, or get delivery, with outdoor seating when the weather is good.

Our Story

Star's Cafe began in 2022, in Nalasopara East — a small, warm, cosy veg cafe where friends and families could sit together over pizza, coffee and freakshakes.

That is still what it is today: a simple neighbourhood cafe, open 9am to 11pm every day, where a meal for two costs about ₹350, and where birthdays, evening chai and lazy weekend lunches all find a table. Dine in, take away, or get it delivered — you are always welcome here.
